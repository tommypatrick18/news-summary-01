The House voted 222-209 Wednesday to end the record 43-day shutdown; Trump signed the bill that night funding the government through Jan. 30 without extending ACA subsidies (AP). Russia and Ukraine ended US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi, their first in nine months, agreeing to swap up to 1,500 prisoners each; Kushner and Witkoff head to Moscow Thursday (Reuters). Iran's army said a tanker caught fire after hitting naval mines east of Hormuz Wednesday; UKMTO reported an incident but did not confirm a mine, fire or casualties (Xinhua/UKMTO). At COP31, 102 leaders met in Antalya as UN chief Guterres warned of a "path to mutually assured destruction"; Brazil and Turkey said wars must not distract from climate (Reuters). NATO jets scrambled after at least four drones entered Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia; Lithuania closed Vilnius and Kaunas airports (Reuters/LRT). A Chinese university with military ties bought two blacklisted Nvidia H200 chips for about $123,000 (Reuters). Nvidia's board met late on CEO Jensen Huang's fate amid an affair-and-espionage scandal and FBI probe; shares fell over 8% after hours as a senator urged him to resign (Reuters). Storms flooded Gaza camps for over 940,000 displaced, with 150,000 tents needed; Israeli strikes killed at least five Palestinians, including two in a Sheikh Radwan tent strike (Guardian/BBC/Al Jazeera). DRC data put the Bundibugyo outbreak at 11,042 cases and at least 5,387 deaths, the DRC's deadliest; Kenya discharged its last patient Wednesday after 9 cases (7 recovered, 2 died), starting a 42-day countdown (Reuters/WHO).
