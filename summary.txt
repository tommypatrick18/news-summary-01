Russia and Ukraine concluded two days of U.S.-brokered peace talks in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday, agreeing to a major swap of up to 1,500 prisoners each as mediators prepared to head to Moscow despite unresolved territorial disputes (Reuters). At the COP31 summit in Antalya, 102 heads of state gathered as UN chief Antonio Guterres warned that the climate crisis is on a "path to mutually assured destruction" while the leaders of Brazil and Turkey urged that global wars not distract from environmental action (Reuters). Heavy storms flooded camps in Gaza sheltering over 940,000 displaced people, with charities warning that 150,000 tents are urgently needed as thousands currently lack shelter (Guardian/BBC). The Democratic Republic of Congo reported that a Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak has caused at least 5,387 deaths out of 11,042 cases, becoming the deadliest such outbreak in the country's history and the second-largest on record (government data). Finally, procurement records showed a Chinese university with military ties purchased two blacklisted Nvidia H200 AI chips for roughly $123,000 despite U.S. export restrictions (Reuters).
