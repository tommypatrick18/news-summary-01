President Donald Trump signed a bill Wednesday night ending the record 43-day shutdown after a 222-209 House vote; it funds the government through January 30 but does not extend ACA health subsidies (AP). Russia and Ukraine agreed to swap up to 1,500 prisoners each after US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi, their first in nine months (Reuters). Iran's army said a tanker caught fire after hitting naval mines Wednesday east of the Strait of Hormuz; UKMTO reported an incident but did not confirm a mine, fire or casualties (Xinhua/UKMTO). At COP31, 102 leaders heard UN chief Guterres warn of a "path to mutually assured destruction," while Brazil and Turkey said wars must not distract from climate (Reuters). NATO jets scrambled after at least four drones entered Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia; Lithuania closed Vilnius and Kaunas airports (Reuters/LRT). A Chinese university with military ties bought two blacklisted Nvidia H200 chips for about $123,000 (Reuters). Nvidia's board met into the night on CEO Jensen Huang's fate amid an affair-and-espionage scandal and FBI probe, shares fell over 8% after hours (WSJ/CBS). Wang Yi held a "positive and constructive" call with Marco Rubio ahead of an expected Xi-Trump meeting at the Shenzhen APEC summit (Reuters/Xinhua). Storms flooded Gaza camps for over 940,000 displaced people, with 150,000 tents needed (Guardian/BBC); Israeli strikes killed at least five Palestinians, including two in a Sheikh Radwan tent strike (Al Jazeera). DRC government data put the Bundibugyo outbreak at 11,042 cases and at least 5,387 deaths, the DRC's deadliest; Kenya discharged its last patient Wednesday after 9 cases (7 recovered, 2 died), starting a 42-day countdown Thursday (Reuters/WHO). Anthropic's bankers may raise its IPO price above $78; the valuation could reach $1.5 trillion or more (Financial Times). October CPI rose 0.4%, making a December Fed rate hike nearly certain, with an 87.3% chance of a 25bp rise (Reuters).
